Title: Pocketsun

Short description:
Small, kind messages that make your day a little lighter. No account, no ads.

Full description:
Pocketsun sends you a small, kind message now and then. Not many, not pushy – just a friendly thought at the right moment.

How it works
• Choose how many messages you want per day (1–5) and during which hours.
• Messages arrive as quiet notifications, and you can add a widget to your home screen.
• Over 150 English messages in four kinds: warm, humor, small actions and perspective.
• Write your own messages – they are mixed in with the rest.
• Give a thumbs up or down, save favorites and hide what you don't like.
• Pause for an hour, the rest of the day or a week when you need quiet.

Privacy first
• No account, no sign-in and no ads.
• Everything you create and choose stays on your phone.
• The app only uses the internet if you tap "Send feedback", and then only anonymous numbers are sent – never message text.
• You can delete all your data in settings at any time.

Pocketsun is made to make everyday life a little better. It is not treatment or medical help. If you need help, contact your doctor or local health services.
